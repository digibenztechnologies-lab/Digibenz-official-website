Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

How do you select creators?

We consider audience relevance, content style, platform, goals and practical fit. Your team approves the final selection.

Can you guarantee reach or sales?

No. Creator and platform performance varies. We report campaign activity without promising outcomes.

Who pays creator fees?

Creator fees and production costs are separate unless a proposal explicitly includes them.

Can creators make the content?

Yes. Creator-produced content can be included with agreed briefs, review and usage terms.

Do you support one-off campaigns?

Yes. We can coordinate a single campaign or recurring creator partnerships.
