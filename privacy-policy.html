How Digibenz Technologies collects and uses information. These website policies do not replace a client proposal or service agreement.

1. Introduction

Digibenz Technologies explains how information is handled when you visit digibenz.com, submit an enquiry, contact us or use our services.

2. Information collected

We may collect name, email, mobile number, business name, industry, website and project requirements, plus technical and usage data such as IP address, browser, device, pages visited and traffic source where available. We collect only information reasonably needed for the purpose.

3. Collection methods

Information may come from contact, quote and consultation forms; WhatsApp, email and telephone; client onboarding; and cookies or analytics where enabled.

4. Purpose

We use information to answer enquiries, prepare proposals, deliver and support services, manage campaigns and reporting, administer billing, improve the site, send permitted business updates, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations.

5. Cookies and tracking

Essential storage supports site functions. Analytics and advertising technologies are separate and should be used only when configured and any required consent is obtained. Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel and conversion tracking are not claimed as active unless installed.

6. Providers

Hosting, email, form, cloud, CRM, analytics, advertising, payment and technical providers may process data as needed. Each provider is subject to its own terms and applicable arrangements.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information for independent marketing. We may share it with authorized service providers, when you direct us, to protect security or rights, or where law requires.

8. Retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably needed for business, contract, accounting, legal and dispute purposes. Reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards are used; absolute security cannot be promised.

9. Your choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent where relevant, opt out of promotional communications or raise a concern. Identity verification or lawful retention may apply.

10. External links and children

Third-party sites have their own privacy practices. Our professional services are not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect children’s data for independent marketing.
