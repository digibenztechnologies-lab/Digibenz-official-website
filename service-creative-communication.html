Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

What does brand communication include?

It can include positioning, messaging, tone of voice, visual direction and campaign communication, scoped to your needs.

Can you refresh an existing brand?

Yes. We review what is working, identify inconsistencies and plan a focused refresh.

Do you create campaign concepts as well as designs?

Yes. Creative concepts and communication direction can be included in the agreed scope.

How do you keep creative work consistent?

We agree on brand foundations and usage direction, then apply them across selected channels.

Can you support one campaign only?

Yes. We can scope a standalone campaign or a longer-term communication plan.
